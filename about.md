# EnjoyChinese - Learn Mandarin Chinese

EnjoyChinese is a user-friendly mobile application built for non-native speakers who want to learn Mandarin Chinese efficiently. Whether you are a beginner with zero Chinese foundation or a learner who wants to consolidate your vocabulary and pronunciation, this app provides practical learning materials tailored for you.

## About EnjoyChinese
This app focuses on practical daily Chinese. It covers common scenarios including greetings, shopping, transportation, dining and traveling. Every word and sentence comes with native audio pronunciation, so you can listen and repeat anytime to improve your listening and speaking skills.

Instead of rigid textbook-style learning, EnjoyChinese adopts lightweight learning sessions. You can study for a few minutes every day on your phone, which fits the schedule of overseas learners.

## EnjoyCards: Companion Flashcard App
EnjoyCards is the supporting flashcard app designed for vocabulary review. It works perfectly with EnjoyChinese.
With spaced repetition algorithm, EnjoyCards helps you review Chinese words at the best time, so you can memorize vocabulary longer with less effort.

Official website: [enjoychinese.app](https://enjoychinese.app)
Flashcard app: [enjoycards.app](https://enjoycards.app)

## Core Features
- Native speaker audio for all Chinese sentences and vocabulary
- Scenario-based practical Chinese lessons
- Simple, clean interface for easy self-study
- Sync vocabulary data with EnjoyCards for review
- Suitable for learners outside of China

## Who is this app for?
Foreign students, expats, language lovers, travelers and anyone who is interested in learning authentic Mandarin Chinese.
